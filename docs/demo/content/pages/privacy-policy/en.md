This site is the notACMS project site. Here is what data it collects and why.

## Cookies

This site sets one functional cookie:

| Cookie | Purpose | Expires |
|---|---|---|
| `cookie_consent` | Remembers that you accepted this notice | 365 days |
| `notacms-theme` | Remembers your light/dark theme preference | Persistent (localStorage) |

No tracking cookies. No third-party scripts except Phosphor Icons via CDN.

## Analytics

This site uses [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/). It collects anonymous aggregate data (page views, referrers, country-level location) without cookies, without fingerprinting, and without processing personal data. No data is shared with advertisers.
