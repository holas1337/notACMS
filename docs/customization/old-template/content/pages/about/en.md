## What I do

Most of my time is spent in woodland. I survey trees, record species, map habitats, and lead guided walks for naturalists and beginners alike. I care about observation that is patient enough to see what is actually there, notes that are detailed enough to be useful a decade later, and walks that do not end at the first viewpoint.

I also spend time working with conservation trusts — writing habitat assessments, helping with veteran tree surveys, and documenting the things that are easy to overlook when a woodland is managed for production rather than ecology.

## Background

I am a field naturalist with a focus on woodland ecology and dendrology. Over the past several years I have surveyed everything from small urban copses to larger ancient woodland complexes.

I enjoy sharing what I find — writing about identification, contributing records to national databases, and learning by walking the same ground in different seasons.

Outside of the forest: maps, natural history illustration, and occasional deep dives into topics that have nothing to do with trees.
