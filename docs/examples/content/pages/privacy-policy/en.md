This site is a demo template. Here is what data it collects and why.

## Contact form

The [contact page](/contact/) collects your name, email, website (optional), subject, and message. This data is sent directly to an email inbox via SMTP. It is not stored in a database, shared with third parties, or used for marketing.

**Legal basis:** legitimate interest (replying to your message). You provide this data voluntarily.

## Cookies

This site sets two cookies:

| Cookie | Purpose | Expires |
|---|---|---|
| `cookie_consent` | Remembers that you accepted this notice | 365 days |
| `lang` | Remembers your language preference | Session (when browser closes) |

No tracking cookies, no analytics cookies.

## Bot protection

The [contact page](/contact/) uses [Cloudflare Turnstile](https://www.cloudflare.com/products/turnstile/) instead of a traditional CAPTCHA. Turnstile may process browser signals to distinguish humans from bots. See [Cloudflare's Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for details.
